Children's Safety Comes First

We are taking planned safety measures to help children participate safely and responsibly.

🚓

Police / Traffic Coordination

Police/traffic coordination is planned, subject to confirmation, to help manage vehicles and support safety along the run route.

🚗

Lead Safety Vehicle

A designated support car is planned to travel well ahead of the children to help clear the path, set a safe pace, and protect the running group.

🛡️

Organized Supervision

Dedicated Vibrun volunteers and adult organizers will supervise and run alongside children throughout the event.

🪢

Safety Ropes & Barriers

Safety ropes will be held along appropriate flanks of the route to help children remain inside the running area.

🏛️

Civic / Municipal Coordination

Municipal/corporation coordination is planned to assist with route cleanliness, clearance, and overall civic support.

How the Run Works

A simple overview of the event flow for families.

01

Registration

A parent or legal guardian registers the child using the form on this page.

02

Arrival

Participants arrive at the start point before the 5:00 AM start time.

03

Safety Briefing

Children are organized into the supervised running group before the start.

04

The Run

Children run within the designated safety formation along the planned route.

05

Finish

The group reaches the designated finish point at Laxmi Palace.

Parent FAQ

Answers based on the information currently shared by organizers.

Only a parent or legal guardian may register a child. Consent is required before submission.
The registration form accepts children aged 4 to 18 years.
The run is planned for Sunday at 5:00 AM. The exact calendar date will be confirmed by the organizers.
The listed starting point is Kekri Khatu Shyam Mandir / Poki Nadi / Mannat Dhaba.
The listed finish point is Laxmi Palace, Kekri.
The route runs from the start point to Laxmi Palace. Use the map on this page for orientation. Exact distance is still to be confirmed.
Planned measures include adult supervision, a lead safety vehicle, safety ropes/barriers, civic coordination, and police/traffic coordination subject to confirmation.
Register in advance, arrive before the start time, and share any essential safety notes in the form. Further instructions will be shared by organizers if needed.
